# Regression Assumptions

Recently was discussing with a friend (Mashrur bhai) about the assumptions for regression, and I thought it would be useful to write down some of the key points. There are essentially two approaches we can introduce the assumptions, 

- Writing the linear regression model first and then imposing the assumptions on the error term (this is the most common approach in undergraduate textbooks), or
- Starting with the CEF (Conditional Expectation Function) and then deriving the assumptions from there (This approach is more common in graduate textbooks. You can look at the the beautiful book by **Hansen** <d-cite key="hansen_2022"></d-cite>, and also the book by **Angrist and Pischke** <d-cite key="angrist_2009a"></d-cite> and the excellent notes by **Chaisemartin**<d-cite key="dechaisemartin_"></d-cite>).
